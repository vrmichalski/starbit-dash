Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Support

Summary

Starbit Dash does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, pilot level, upgrades, quests, settings, local high scores and the pilot name you choose) is saved only on your iPhone. It is never sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. Device backups you make with iCloud or a computer may contain it; these backups are handled by Apple or by you.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is sent to the Game Center leaderboard together with your Game Center profile. This service is run by Apple. I only see the leaderboard that Apple shows to every player. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can use the game without Game Center.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash reporting tools of third parties and does not track you across apps or websites. Crash reports that you choose to share with developers in the iPhone settings are provided by Apple in anonymous form.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because no personal data is collected, no data of children is collected either.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since I do not hold any personal data about you, there is usually nothing to disclose or delete. Feel free to contact me with any question.

Changes

If the app changes, for example through in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before the new version is released. The date at the top shows the current version.
