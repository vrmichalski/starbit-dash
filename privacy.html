Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The app

Starbit Dash does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not send data to me.

Your progress, settings and pilot name are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time with Settings → Reset all data, or by deleting the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple together with your Game Center profile and shown on the leaderboard. Apple is responsible for this; Apple's privacy policy applies. You can play without Game Center.

E-mail

If you e-mail me, I use your address and message only to reply (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them once your request is settled. The mailbox is provided by Apple (iCloud Mail).

This website

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To use these rights, e-mail me.

Changes

If the app starts to process personal data, for example through in-app purchases, this policy will be updated first.
